About Me ​

I’m a Jamaican-born entrepreneur with a diverse international background. After moving to Trinidad and Tobago in 2001 and then relocating to the United States in 2019, I am returning Jamaica in 2025. My focus will be on launching new international ventures in Finance and Open Source Software.

Professional Journey ​

As an entrepreneur, I thrive on identifying challenges and crafting innovative solutions. My background in computer science, finance, management, and mechanics equips me with a unique perspective that drives my success. I have a proven track record in project management, characterized by a logical approach and a results-driven mindset. I take pride in my ability to meet operational goals through hard work and resilience.
